The Problem

When the books slip, compliance, tenants and cash flow slip with them.

Rent collection, maintenance costs, owner distributions and regulatory compliance all land on the same desk while you're trying to grow. When records get mixed between properties, staying audit-ready gets harder every month.

Tenant ledgers fall out of date, so no one is sure who's paid and who's late. Repair bills get coded to the wrong property, owner statements take longer to explain, and suddenly you're asking the question every manager dreads: where did all the cash go?

Complete Controller keeps all three on track.

Your team keeps every property's records clean and separate, every tenant ledger current, and every owner statement clear, so cash flow becomes something you can plan around.

Questions

What owners and managers ask us.

I manage multiple properties. Can you keep each one separate?

Yes. We track every dollar by property, unit or full portfolio, so you get performance insights at every level with no mixing numbers.

Can you help me stay on top of rent collections?

Yes. We keep rent rolls and tenant ledgers accurate and up to date, so you always know who's paid, who's late and what needs attention. Need help with rents or tracking in other software? Just ask. We can discuss how to support the systems you already use.

Our owners want clear monthly reports. Can you make them easier to understand?

Yes. We prepare clean, clear owner statements that highlight the essentials: income, expenses, distributions and property health.

Can you help us track maintenance costs accurately?

Yes. We categorize and allocate maintenance and repair expenses so you can see true property profitability.

How safe is tenant and financial data with you?

Your data is encrypted, protected and stored in secure U.S.-based cloud environments.

I already use AppFolio or Buildium. Do I have to switch tools?

No. We work with AppFolio, Buildium, QuickBooks and other property management systems. You keep your tools and we make your numbers stronger.

We have in-house staff. Do you replace them or support them?

We support them. Many firms keep their internal bookkeeper or admin team and use us for controller-level review, accuracy checks, cleanup and oversight, so they stay audit-ready and year-end is much cleaner.

How much does property management bookkeeping cost?

Pricing is transparent and sized to your workload, number of properties and reporting needs. Plans start at $165 a month (billed quarterly) for Minimal, with Basic at $405 a month and Complete at $970 a month, plus a one-time setup fee.
